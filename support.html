DR Voice Type Support

Questions or problems? Email dr.labs.contact@gmail.com — include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I turn on the keyboard?

Settings app → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → DR Voice Type. Then tap DR Voice Type in that list and turn on Allow Full Access (needed for voice only). In any app, hold or tap the globe key to switch to DR Voice Type.

Why does the app open when I tap the mic?

iOS doesn't let keyboards use the microphone, so the DR Voice Type app opens for a moment to start listening. Go back to what you were writing (the "<" link at the top left, or swipe right along the bottom edge) and keep talking. The session stays on for the length you choose in Settings, so you only do this once.

How do I stop listening?

Tap "Mic off" in the keyboard. The session also ends on its own after the length set in Settings.

The cleanup isn't changing much

The free version uses basic cleanup (fillers, "scratch that", spoken punctuation). With Pro, cleanup uses your iPhone's built-in AI when it's available and turned on (Settings app → Apple Intelligence & Siri). DR Voice Type → Settings → Cleanup shows which one is active. For the most accurate cleanup, Pro lets you paste your own Claude API key under "Upgrade: Claude AI polish".

What's free and what's Pro?

Free: the full keyboard, swipe typing and next-word suggestions, plus 10 voice dictations a day with basic cleanup. Pro is a one-time purchase (no subscription) that adds unlimited dictation and AI cleanup. Already bought Pro on another device? Tap Restore Purchase in Settings.

Where do I get a Claude API key?

At console.anthropic.com → API Keys. Add a little credit to your Anthropic account; a dictation usually costs well under a cent.

The keyboard doesn't show up in some apps

Password fields always use Apple's keyboard, and some work-managed apps block other keyboards. That's set by iOS or your organization, not by DR Voice Type.

Privacy

See the privacy policy. In short: no accounts, no tracking, audio is never saved, and everything stays on your phone unless you add your own Claude key.
